Zone Buff
Awakens any Elemental Type Zones or a Damage Type Attack Zones or some Hybrid Zones:
  • Applicable Hybrid Zones are Double Strike Stance Attack Zones, & Moonlight Stance
  • Doubles damage multiplicators for Zones:
    • to +60% for Attack Zones and Thunder, Crystal, Shade (other elements) Type Zones
    • to +100% for Fire, Water, Wind, Earth and Shade Type Zones
    • Doubles "damage on weakness/resist" effects for Crystal/Shade Type Zone respectively
  • Zone cannot be overwritten by other zones during the duration and duration cannot be extended by attempting to reactivate Another Zone
  • Zone can still be removed/broken by skills that remove zones
  • After duration ends, zone is removed
  • Support Effects can be used at the same time as Another Zone
  • Via active skills, can be activated only once per character
    • When there is no appropriate zone or the zone is already awakened, using a skill that can awaken Zone will not consume awakening ability
    • Non-active skill sources may activate it more than once per battle if explicitly stated
