Description
A young female samurai from the east. She left her home on a secret mission to find her older brother, who she reveres more than anyone in the world. She doesn't rate her skills compared to her brother, but the truth is she's an expert with a blade. Her underestimation of her own skill sometimes gets her into trouble.
