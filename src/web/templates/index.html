About this demo

Select exact character forms you own, or choose the optional twenty-form sample. Normal, Another Style and Extra Style are separate forms. At least one available form is required; fewer than six is allowed because permanently free forms may be added automatically.

  • Only selected owned sidekicks are sent.
  • Stellar Awakening and Light/Shadow are not collected. Equipment uses late-game catalog assumptions, not confirmed item ownership.
  • Recommendations are legal backend candidates, not battle-tested teams or win predictions. Evidence covers inspected sources; missing evidence remains unknown.
  • Celestial Mushroom is the only supported recommendation boss. Rotte Rivel remains a diagnostic case. Graph Q&A is a separate workflow.
